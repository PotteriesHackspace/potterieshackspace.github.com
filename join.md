## Membership enquiries

Potteries Hackspace is dormant and has no usable workshop. There are no regular open nights, and we cannot offer workshop access, project storage or use of equipment.

Current paid membership arrangements, fees and benefits have not been confirmed. The old joining form and subscription amounts are no longer presented as current offers.

For enquiries about the group, see our [Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/potteries-hackspace). An enquiry does not sign you up for paid membership.

Our [historical archive](/archive) records the group's activities and earlier membership announcements. These describe the arrangements at the time, rather than what is available today.
